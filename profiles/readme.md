# Hoan Kiem Lake (Hồ Gươm)

> "The heart of Hanoi, where legend meets modern life."

## 📍 Overview
* **Location:** Historic center of Hanoi, adjacent to the Old Quarter.
* **Area:** ~12 hectares
* **Depth:** 1.2 – 2 meters
* **Other Names:** *Hồ Lục Thủy* (Green Water Lake), *Hồ Tả Vọng*

---

## 🐉 Legend
The name **Hoan Kiem** (meaning "Lake of the Returned Sword") is tied to a 15th-century legend: 
* Emperor **Lê Lợi** (Le Thai To) received a magical sword (*Thuận Thiên*) from the Golden Turtle God to defeat Ming invaders.
* After winning the war, while boating on the lake, a giant golden turtle surfaced and asked him to return the sword to the Dragon King.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks
* **Turtle Tower (*Tháp Rùa*):** A small, mossy, ancient 3-story tower sitting on a small island in the middle of the lake.
* **The Huc Bridge (*Cầu Thê Húc*):** A striking, classic red wooden bridge with a curved design leading to Jade Island.
* **Ngoc Son Temple (*Đền Ngọc Sơn*):** Located on Jade Island at the northern part of the lake, dedicated to national heroes like General Trần Hưng Đạo.

---

## 🍃 Activities & Vibe
* **Morning:** Locals gather for exercise, yoga, jogging, and traditional *Tai Chi* around the shores.
* **Weekend:** The surrounding streets turn into a vibrant **pedestrian walking zone** with street art, music, and local games.
* **Treats:** Don't miss trying local **Tràng Tiền ice cream** nearby.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start / Visit Info
* **Best time to visit:** Early morning or late afternoon / weekend evenings.
* **Admission (Ngoc Son Temple):** Open daily (approx. 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM; ticket required for the temple).
